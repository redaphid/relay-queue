Settings

Speech model

Which whisper engine transcribes your dictation. Bigger models are more accurate and slower. All of them are running already, so switching is instant — it only changes which one is asked.

Hear the difference

Record one phrase and every model transcribes that same recording. You get the real numbers for your voice, your room and this machine — which is the only way to pick a point on the accuracy-versus-speed trade honestly.

Dictation timing

When dictation decides you have finished speaking. These are the knobs worth turning by feel: if it keeps cutting you off mid-thought, or never stops listening in a noisy room, they are why.

Saved on the server in data/settings.json, so these travel with the machine rather than the browser — the same settings apply from your phone and your desktop.
